# Ore Conversion

In the <ref item="anvilcraft:large_cauldron"/>, a little **molten gem** works as a catalyst, and the molten metal "regrows" into the matching ore:

- **10mB Molten Ruby** + 250mB molten metal → 1 **deepslate** ore
- **10mB Molten Sapphire** + 250mB molten metal → 1 **stone** ore

Metals and their results:

| Molten Metal | Deepslate Ore | Ore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Molten Iron | <ref item="minecraft:deepslate_iron_ore"/> | <ref item="minecraft:iron_ore"/> |
| Molten Gold | <ref item="minecraft:deepslate_gold_ore"/> | <ref item="minecraft:gold_ore"/> |
| Molten Copper | <ref item="minecraft:deepslate_copper_ore"/> | <ref item="minecraft:copper_ore"/> |
| Molten Tungsten | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_tungsten_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Lead | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_lead_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Silver | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_silver_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Tin | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_tin_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Zinc | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_zinc_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Titanium | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_titanium_ore"/> | — |
| Molten Uranium | <ref item="anvilcraft:deepslate_uranium_ore"/> | — |

<tip>
AnvilCraft's metals only generate as **deepslate** ores, so apart from vanilla Iron, Gold and Copper none of them has a stone variant.
</tip>
