# Melting

Use **Super Heating** in the <ref item="anvilcraft:large_cauldron"/> to turn material into fluid:

- 1 metal block or 9 gems → 1000mB of the matching molten fluid
- In addition:
- 9 <ref item="minecraft:quartz"/> → 1000mB Molten Quartz
- 9 <ref item="minecraft:amethyst_shard"/> → 1000mB Molten Amethyst

# Cooling

Fill a cauldron with molten fluid and let an <ref item="minecraft:anvil"/> drop on it; the melt solidifies back into the **matching block item**, and the cauldron is emptied.

<recipe id="anvilcraft_fluid:solid_liquid/cooling/iron"/>
<recipe id="anvilcraft_fluid:solid_liquid/cooling/lead"/>

<tip>
The cauldron has to be completely full; with anything less, dropping the anvil shapes nothing.
</tip>
