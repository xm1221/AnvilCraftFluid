# Frost Fluid

Washes **every enchantment** off an item.

- In the <ref item="anvilcraft:large_cauldron"/>, each enchantment washed off costs 250mB of Frost Fluid
- The washed-off enchantments become **Liquid Enchantment**: level 1 costs 1mB, level 2 costs 2mB, level 3 costs 4mB, level 4 costs 8mB, level 5 costs 16mB, and so on
- An enchanted book that has been washed empty turns back into a plain <ref item="minecraft:book"/>
- Standing in it is **like standing in powder snow**: freezing builds up and causes frost damage

# Ember Fluid

Repairs **Ember Metal gear** on its own, just like standing in lava but far faster.

- An item **standing in Ember Fluid** is repaired bit by bit, much faster than in lava — pour it into a cauldron or just spill it on the ground
- The fluid itself is not consumed; it works on contact
- Only gear made of Ember Metal that can be reforged responds

- Flowing into **Frost Fluid** it solidifies: when both sides are **source** blocks it forms <ref item="minecraft:blackstone"/>; any other combination (including a source meeting a flow) forms <ref item="minecraft:stone"/>
- For how it is made, see [Fluid-based Recipes](100_anvilcraft_fluid/012_alloy.md)

# Cursed Gold Fluid

Molten gold that holds a **curse**; it is a molten metal itself and can be cooled into <ref item="anvilcraft:cursed_gold_block"/>.

- In the Large Cauldron, each curse washed off costs 500mB of Molten Gold and produces the same amount of Cursed Gold Fluid
- Standing in it inflicts **Weakness**

# Redstone-Resin Colloid

A resin colloid mixed with redstone dust. It flows, and when powered by a redstone signal it strongly powers chargeable blocks and activates redstone components it touches; its flow is blocked by most redstone components.
