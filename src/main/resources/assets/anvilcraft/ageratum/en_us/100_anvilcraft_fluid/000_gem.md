# Molten Gems

The molten form of gems, 6 in total: Ruby, Quartz, Sapphire, Topaz, Emerald and Amethyst.

- 9 gem **items** melt into one bucket (1000mB) of the matching molten gem
- Molten gems are the catalyst for [ore conversion](100_anvilcraft_fluid/011_ore.md); the **red, yellow, blue and green** ones can also be used to make [Molten Royal Steel](100_anvilcraft_fluid/012_alloy.md)

<tip>
- Each molten gem forms a different block when it meets water: Ruby gives Granite, Molten Quartz gives Diorite, the other gems give Andesite, and Amethyst gives Calcite.
</tip>
