# Molten Metals

The molten form of metals, 11 in total: Iron, Gold, Copper, Tungsten, Royal Steel, Lead, Silver, Tin, Zinc, Titanium and Uranium.

- One metal **block** melts into one bucket (1000mB) of the matching molten metal
- Molten metals are the main ingredient of [ore conversion](100_anvilcraft_fluid/011_ore.md) (250mB of melt makes one ore)
- Molten Iron is used to make Molten Royal Steel and Blocks of Magnet
- Molten Tungsten can be used to make netherite, turning Netherite Scrap back into [Ancient Debris](100_anvilcraft_fluid/013_time_warp.md)
- The radiation of Molten Uranium inflicts **Wither**

<tip>
The source block of a molten metal forms the matching ore when it meets water; a **flowing** molten metal does not react to water.
</tip>
